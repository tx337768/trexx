# 🎯 Noble Meridian Competitive Research: Executive Summary

**Current State:** ~2 sales/day at $145-$195 price point  
**Goal:** 5-10× growth (10+ sales/day within 12 weeks)  
**Research Date:** November 12, 2026

---

## 🚨 CRITICAL FINDINGS

### What's Killing Conversions Right Now:

1. **❌ No Clear Free Shipping Message** 
   - Thursday Boots screams it everywhere: "FREE SHIPPING & RETURNS"
   - Noble Meridian: Hidden/unclear
   - **Impact:** 5-10% conversion loss

2. **❌ Discount-First Positioning**
   - Homepage leads with "Up to 60% off" 
   - Trains customers to wait for sales
   - Competitors lead with craft/value story

3. **❌ Zero Video Creative**
   - Thursday Boots: 4,800 active ads, heavy UGC video
   - Noble Meridian: Static images only
   - **Impact:** Algorithm deprioritizes, low engagement

4. **❌ Weak Social Proof**
   - Beckett Simonon: "Hundreds of thousands of products sold"
   - Noble Meridian: Trust badges only, no volume metrics

5. **❌ Mobile Friction**
   - Black Friday banner + popup = 2 overlays before browsing
   - Competitors: 1 overlay max

## ⚡ 12-WEEK GROWTH PLAN

### PHASE 1: Quick Wins (Weeks 1-2) — $500 Investment
**Goal:** 2/day → 3-4/day (+50-100%)

✅ Add "FREE SHIPPING & RETURNS" to header  
✅ Change homepage headline to value narrative (not discount)  
✅ Add social proof widget ("Join 10,000+ customers")  
✅ Remove either Black Friday banner OR popup (not both)  
✅ Launch micro UGC campaign (email 50 customers for photos)

---

### PHASE 2: Paid Scale (Weeks 3-8) — $3,100 Investment
**Goal:** 3-4/day → 5-6/day (+25-50%)

✅ Produce 5 UGC testimonial videos ($500-$1,000)  
✅ Launch Meta ads test: $50/day, Add-to-Cart objective  
   - 3 UGC videos + 3 product carousels  
   - Interest targeting: Thursday Boots, Allen Edmonds, GQ  
   - Expected ROAS: 2.0-3.0×  
✅ Launch retargeting: $20/day, 30-day website visitors  
   - Expected ROAS: 4.0-6.0×

---

### PHASE 3: CRO (Weeks 6-12) — $1,500 Investment
**Goal:** 5-6/day → 8-10/day (+30-60%)

✅ A/B test email capture AOV gate (10% off $200+ vs. free gift)  
✅ Create "Archive Sale" section ($99-$129 entry point)  
✅ Replace Nov 30 timer with evergreen urgency (inventory scarcity)  
✅ Launch referral program ("Give $20, Get $20")

## ⚠️ RISKS TO WATCH

1. **If free shipping isn't currently offered:** Adding it = 5-8% margin hit ($8-$14/order). Offset by raising email discount gate to $200+.

2. **If ad pixel has <50 purchases:** Lookalike audiences won't work. Rely on interest targeting + retargeting only.

3. **If return rate is high:** Paid scale amplifies problems. Fix product/sizing issues before scaling ads.
