News · Timed clearance · Dress shoes above £280

Eighteen nights left. After 11.59pm in London on 30 November, the £306 shoe stops costing £122.

Noble Meridian has printed a closing time on its end-of-season collection: 11.59pm London time, Monday 30 November 2026. Full-grain Oxfords, loafers and Chelsea boots — list prices between £285 and £402 — sit at 60% off on the models that matter. The site shows no second date.

By James Hartley, Fashion & Retail Correspondent · London · Prices checked on the brand’s catalogue, 12 November 2026

Calendar note Black Friday falls on 27 November. This clearance does not end that Friday. It ends the following Monday at 11.59pm. Anyone waiting “until after Friday” arrives late.

This is not a ten-per-cent voucher. It is a city shoe listed at three hundred pounds, cut by more than half — with the hour written out. Today, Thursday 12 November, eighteen nights remain. Each night that passes is one fewer night on which £122.49 still buys what the list price marks at £306.23.

The Bolton Chelsea, in full-grain leather, falls from £306.23 to £122.49. The Yates Oxford, from £323.46 to £129.38. The Leonard monk strap, from £401.93 to £160.77. On each of those pairs the arithmetic is sixty per cent. In sterling: £184, £194 and £241 that leave the card — or return to it, if the order is placed after the 30th.

“30 November, 11.59pm, London time.” One date. No extension announced. The cost of waiting is the list price.

Why the list price sits above £280

A pair at £122 is only a bargain if the pair at £306 was a category price. The catalogue shows both figures on the same variant. Full-grain leather on the boot and chukka; calfskin on the tassel loafer. On several models the sole is a choice — leather or rubber. The usual size run runs from US 7 to US 14.

Worn twice a week, the Bolton at £122 works out below £1.20 a wear in the first year. At list price the same sum tops £2.90. After the 30th, the higher figure is the one that returns.

Three decisions. Not one hundred and thirty.

The public collection holds about 130 models. Opening every one costs the deadline. Three uses settle the choice:

City, jeans, blazer. Bolton Chelsea, £122.49. No laces. Black, bordeaux, oak, tan or brown.

Office. Yates Oxford, £129.38 — or the Valencia wholecut, £141.63, when a cleaner upper line matters more than the day’s sole.

Slip-on with detail. Bernard, £137.81. Tassels, calfskin. The list is £344.51. The gap, £206.70, nearly buys another pair on this page.
